# Split Second v3: script

The rule a viewer learns in the first 20 seconds: **you get a split second.** Every decision runs on a timer that shrinks from 5 s at D1 to 2 s at D4. Hesitate and the Hound has you. Nothing on screen marks the right choice.

Winning path about 100 s. First choice at about 23 s. Each capture about 10 s, then FLATLINED. Dialogue is captions, plus Dex's one off-screen line in the setup (the audited v2 take).

## Logic walk

- **Why does the Hound find her?** He's a hunter, and he corners her wherever the space closes in: the vent, the alley, the carriage. Open ground keeps her alive. A viewer can feel the pattern by D3; nothing says it.
- **What decides each choice?** The timer. It shrinks every round, and running out of time is a capture.
- **Why is the Hound on the train?** Beat 17 shows him leaping onto the last car.
- **Why does the van leave without her?** It's racing the train alongside a highway; at the ramp it has to take the exit or be boxed in.
- **Why the roof of the train?** Climbing keeps her in the open, and Dex is driving the highway that runs beside the tracks.
