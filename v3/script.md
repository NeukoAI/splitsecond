# Split Second v3: script

The rule a viewer learns in the first 20 seconds: **the Hound sees through his red drones. Stay out of the red.** Every decision shows one red drone light on the wrong choice.

Winning path about 100 s. First choice at about 23 s. Each capture about 10 s, then FLATLINED. Dialogue is captions, plus Dex's one off-screen line in the setup (the audited v2 take).

## Logic walk

- **Why does the Hound find her?** The drones. Every capture happens where a drone looked. Every escape is where none did.
- **Why is the Hound on the train?** Beat 17 shows him leaping onto the last car.
- **Why does the van leave without her?** It's racing the train alongside a highway; at the ramp it has to take the exit or be boxed in.
- **Why the roof of the train?** The carriage has a drone in it, and Dex is driving the highway that runs beside the tracks.
